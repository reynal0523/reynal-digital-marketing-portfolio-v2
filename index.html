PRACTICAL SAMPLE / CONCEPT

A content plan with a clear purpose.

A short example for a fictional wellness brand. This demonstrates planning and measurement approach; it is not a client campaign or a report of actual results.

CAMPAIGN GOAL

Build awareness, encourage conversation, and make the next step clear.

AUDIENCE

Busy adults interested in simple, practical wellness routines.

CONTENT MIX

Educate · Connect · Invite

MONDAY01 / EDUCATE

3 small ways to reset during a busy week

Format: Carousel or short video

Share three realistic, easy-to-try wellness habits. Keep each tip clear, useful, and easy to save for later.

Caption direction

A busy week doesn't have to mean skipping every pause. Which small reset could you try today?

CTA: Save this for later
WEDNESDAY02 / CONNECT

What helps you recharge?

Format: Static post or story prompt

Invite audience participation with a simple question and a welcoming visual that encourages genuine replies.

Caption direction

Everyone recharges differently. What's one small thing that helps you feel more like yourself?

CTA: Share in the comments
FRIDAY03 / INVITE

Make room for a little more care

Format: Service-led graphic or short video

Explain one service benefit in plain language and guide interested readers toward the next step without pressure.

Caption direction

Want help finding a wellness routine that fits your week? Explore our services and see what feels right for you.

CTA: Explore services

MEASUREMENT PLAN

Track the signal, not just the post.

Compare each post against the campaign goal and the account's own baseline. No performance figures are claimed in this concept.

AwarenessReach, impressions, video views
EngagementComments, shares, saves, engagement rate
ActionProfile visits, link clicks, inquiries
Next stepReview what worked, note context, test one improvement
